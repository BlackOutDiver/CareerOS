Methodology

CareerOS is an evidence-based career portfolio generator. It turns documented operational records into readable professional summaries, dashboards, and share packages.

Source Records

  • Company job records and project numbers
  • Personnel assignments and credited hours
  • Daily manifests and work activities
  • Equipment records
  • Material and production records
  • Client, activity, and project-location metadata

AI-Assisted Processing

AI assistance was used to help organize and summarize the imported records. This includes standardizing company names, grouping similar equipment and material names, identifying infrastructure categories, grouping work activities, and generating a human-readable career synopsis.

AI was not used to invent jobs, fabricate hours, certify credentials, or modify the original company records. The system reports experience from the imported database and applies normalization rules to make the records easier to understand.

Human Review

CareerOS outputs should be reviewed by the employee before distribution. When inconsistencies are found, the normalization rules are corrected so future reports are more consistent.
